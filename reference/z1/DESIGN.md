## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an elevated cultural concierge aesthetic, purposefully crafted for travelers exploring Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar. It bridges the dignified architectural heritage of Aceh—characterized by intricate craftsmanship, deep evergreen landscapes, and heritage gold—with modern, friction-free booking patterns reminiscent of leading international travel products.

The personality is refined, grounded, and welcoming. Interfaces project authority, trust, and cultural depth without sacrificing conversion efficiency or utility. Visual compositions rely on balanced proportions, tactile contrast between rich forest tones and warm cream surfaces, and disciplined editorial typography that elevates travel discovery into a curated journey.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the physical topography and ceremonial heritage of northern Sumatra:

- **Deep Green (`#103629`)**: Acts as the dominant anchor for high-impact navigation bars, primary headings, key containment shells, and structural anchors.
- **Forest Green (`#174B3A`)**: Used as the primary brand touchpoint for interactive active states, secondary container fills, and rich brand badges.
- **Golden Accent (`#E9A23B`)**: Reserved for conversion triggers, promotional flashes, selected tab indicators, rating stars, and high-value cultural verification badges.
- **Warm Cream (`#F7F5EF`)**: The global page canvas, imparting an organic, parchment-like tactility that avoids stark digital sterileness.
- **Neutral White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly designated for interactive cards, input fields, modal dialogs, and booking summary panels to guarantee high contrast.
- **Text Dark (`#26352D`)**: The primary text color for body and UI elements, replacing harsh black with a deeply saturated botanical tone.
- **Slate Muted (`#64748B`)**: Applied exclusively to auxiliary captions, inactive icons, and metadata dividers.
- **Verified Emerald (`#059669`)**: Used to highlight licensed tour operators, official Syariah-compliant certification badges, and successful transaction states.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial grace of `Noto Serif` with the geometric clarity and legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`. 

- Use `Noto Serif` strictly for primary storytelling headers, destination editorial showcases, cultural narratives, and package tier headings. It should never be used for inputs, body copy, or operational transactional tables.
- Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` across the search engine, filter attributes, booking schedules, price tiers, and interactive buttons.
- For monetary displays, format values in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semibold with standardized Indonesian Rupiah styling (`IDR 1.250.000`), maintaining consistent visual baselines across pricing cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a standard 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (max container width 1280px), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet screens (768px - 1024px) and a 4-column fluid stack on mobile devices (<768px).

Booking filter bars and discovery modules sit on an 8px spatial grid. Component density increases around the core booking engine to optimize screen real estate while opening up into generous breathing room across destination editorial sections. 

- Gaps between search engine form fields adhere to `space-sm` (0.5rem) to maintain cohesion as an integrated widget.
- Grid listings for accommodations and tour experiences follow a `space-lg` (1.5rem) separation.
- Section-to-section transitions require `space-2xl` (3rem) on desktop to preserve the elevated cultural aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on ambient environmental shadows tinted with the brand's botanical undertones rather than generic grayscale blurs.

- **Base Cards (Elevation 1)**: Displayed on the `#F7F5EF` canvas using `#FFFFFF` with an ambient shadow of `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(16, 54, 41, 0.06), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(16, 54, 41, 0.04)` paired with a subtle outline of `1px solid rgba(23, 75, 58, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Hover & Dropdown Surfaces (Elevation 2)**: Elevation shifts cleanly to `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(16, 54, 41, 0.10), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 54, 41, 0.05)`, communicating effortless physical response without excessive displacement.
- **Floating Booking Bars & Sticky Navigation (Elevation 3)**: Utilize a surface composed of `#FFFFFF` at 95% opacity with an intense backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) alongside `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(16, 54, 41, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, tailored roundedness level (`roundedness: 1`), keeping edges crisp, structured, and modern. 

- Form fields, primary buttons, and tag chips implement a base radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- Property cards, itinerary blocks, and cultural media modules scale smoothly to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modals, flyout filter panels, and segmented booking control containers utilize `0.75rem` (12px).
- Avoid circular pill buttons for primary booking actions to preserve architectural authority; circular treatments are strictly limited to thumbnail avatars and floating map pins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Search/Book)**: Background `#E9A23B`, text `#26352D` (bold), subtle shadow. On hover, darken to `#D68F29`. Border radius 4px.
- **Secondary Action (Explore/Details)**: Background `#103629`, text `#FFFFFF`. On hover, shift to `#174B3A`.
- **Tertiary (Subtle/Clear)**: Transparent surface, text `#174B3A`, underline on hover.

### Booking & Search Bar Widget
- Single contiguous horizontal unit on desktop, anchored on a `#FFFFFF` surface with an elevation 2 shadow.
- Separated internally with hairline borders using `rgba(23, 75, 58, 0.12)`.
- Input fields display micro-labels (`label-caps`) in `#64748B`, with user selection in `title-md` `#26352D`.

### Cultural Verification & Status Badges
- **Syariah / Halal Certified**: Container `#F7F5EF`, border `1px solid #059669`, text `#059669`, accompanied by a certified seal icon.
- **Heritage & Curated**: Deep green background `#103629`, gold text `#E9A23B`, uppercase `label-caps`.

### Accommodation & Tour Cards
- Composed on pure `#FFFFFF` with image aspects of 16:10.
- Top-right media badges for rating scores: `#103629` container, `#FFFFFF` text, with a gold star icon (`#E9A23B`).
- Footer displays transparent pricing breakdown: localized base rate in small muted type, finalized rate highlighted in bold `title-md` `#103629`.

### Filter Chips
- Unselected: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.25)`, text `#26352D`.
- Active/Selected: Surface `#174B3A`, border `1px solid #174B3A`, text `#FFFFFF` with checkmark icon.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox (4px radius) and round radio borders using `1.5px solid #64748B`.
- Checked state fills with `#103629` and presents a `#FFFFFF` check indicator. Focus states project a `3px` ring of `rgba(233, 162, 59, 0.35)`.
